# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be kind. Argue about the content, not the person.

This is a library of building components; most disagreements here are factual and have an answer —
what a standard actually says, whether a classification code is right, whether a dimension is nominal
or actual. Bring the source. Being right is not a licence to be unpleasant about it, and being
pleasant is not a substitute for being right.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or sexualised language, publishing others' private
information, or persistent disruption after being asked to stop.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, commits, and any space where you are representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report to the repository owner via a
[security advisory](https://github.com/MassingCloud/massing-families/security/advisories/new) (private)
or by contacting [@ibuilder](https://github.com/ibuilder) directly.

Reports are handled confidentially. Responses range from a request to change behaviour, through
removal of comments or commits, to a block — proportionate to what happened and whether it continued
after being raised.
